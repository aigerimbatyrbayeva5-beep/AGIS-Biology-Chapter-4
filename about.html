About this website

The learning website for Chapter 4, Water and Organic Compounds, of the Grade 7 Biology course at Astana Garden International School (IB MYP Sciences, Year 3).

What is here

  • Four interactive presentations (40 minutes each) that zoom between scenes, with activities, vocabulary and teacher-controlled answers.
  • 12 student resources: worksheets, laboratory sheets, English–Russian vocabulary sheets and exit tickets, as PDF to print and DOCX to edit.
  • A Teacher Zone that lists the teacher files and explains how they are kept private.

The unit

Statement of Inquiry: Interactions among substances within living systems support essential functions, and understanding these relationships can guide responsible use of natural resources.

Key concept: Systems · Related concepts: Function, Interaction · Global context: Globalization and sustainability (from the unit planner, proposed — subject to department approval).

Textbook: Biology Grade 7, Chapter 4, pp. 54–63.

Using the site

Devices

Works in current Chrome, Edge, Safari (including iPad) and Firefox. The presentations work best on a laptop, desktop or tablet in landscape; on a phone, turn the phone sideways. On a touch screen, use the control bar at the bottom of a presentation.
