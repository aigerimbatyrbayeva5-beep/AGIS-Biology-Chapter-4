Main activityTEST 1 · CARBOHYDRATES · VIRTUAL DEMO
Iodine test
Проба с йодом
brown→blue-black
Result rule: when iodine solution reacts with carbohydrates (starch), the colour changes from brown to blue or black.
Observe your real samples. Write the colour you see.

Test 1 (≈21:00–23:00). Real samples first if possible. Click Add iodine to model the procedure: (a) cut potato and apple in half, (b) add a few drops of iodine solution, (c) write down the colours.

Expected (textbook result rule): potato turns blue-black → contains carbohydrate (starch). Apple: record the real observation. Ripe apples often show little or no blue-black colour because their carbohydrates are mostly sugars, not starch; unripe apples can show more. The virtual apple therefore shows a “?”.
Precision: the iodine test detects starch, one kind of carbohydrate. The textbook says “carbohydrates” — say “carbohydrate (starch)”.
Main activityTEST 2 · LIPIDS · VIRTUAL DEMO
Paper test
Проба на бумаге
Result rule: if the food contains fat, the traces will be transparent (light passes through).
Which trace is transparent? Hold your real paper up to the light.

Test 2 (≈23:00–25:00). Procedure (textbook p. 61): squeeze and knead potato, olive and apple separately on white paper until wet; clean off food residue; dry 10–15 minutes; compare traces — which one is transparent?

Virtual demo: Squeeze makes three wet spots; Dry speeds up the drying. Water from potato and apple evaporates; the olive’s oil stays.

Expected: olive leaves a transparent (greasy) spot → contains lipid. Potato and apple spots disappear when dry. Students record their real result.
Start the real paper test first (before the iodine and Biuret tests) so it has 10–15 minutes to dry.
Main activityTEST 3 · PROTEINS · VIRTUAL DEMO
Biuret test
Биуретовая проба
blue→purple
Result rule: when Biuret reagent reacts with protein, the colour changes from blue to purple.

Test 3 (≈25:00–27:00). Procedure (textbook p. 61): put 2 ml raw egg white in a test tube, add 2 ml Biuret reagent and mix, write the result. Tube B (water) is an added control — water is in the textbook materials list — so students can compare.

Expected: A turns purple → protein present. B stays blue → no protein.
Biuret reagent is irritant/corrosive. Teacher dispenses; eye protection on; wipe spills immediately.

Exit ticketWORK ALONE · 3 MINUTES
Exit ticket
Билет на выход: ответь сам
1 · Recall
Which reagent tests for protein, and what colour change shows protein?
Biuret reagent: the colour changes from blue to purple.
2 · Understanding
Why does an olive leave a transparent spot on paper, but a potato does not?
The olive contains lipid (fat). Fat does not dry away, so the trace stays transparent. The potato’s trace is water, which dries.
3 · Application
A student adds iodine solution to bread. It turns blue-black. What does this show? Which group is bread in?
The bread contains carbohydrate (starch). Bread is in the carbohydrates group.

Exit ticket (37:00–40:00). Printed slips, alone. Collect.

Success criteria: Q1 names Biuret + blue → purple. Q2 links transparent trace to lipid; water evaporates. Q3 interprets the colour change as evidence of carbohydrate/starch.
